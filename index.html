Frequently asked questions

What can this bot do?

A bot service that automatically handles the daily web Check-In Rewards Event and redeem codes.

Is my account safe?

Yes. The risk is very small, because this is just automation of the game's own official web features. The bot does exactly what you'd do by hand: daily check-in claims and official code redemptions on the official site. It never enters your game, changes nothing, and we only ever need your UID.

Is my UID safe to share?

Yes, it's safe. A UID can only be used to open the store page (daily check-in claims and code redemptions). A UID cannot be used to enter your game, change your password, or move items. Your game login (Google/Facebook/Apple) is completely separate from your store UID. The bot never asks for any password.

What does the bot do with my account?

Only two things: claim daily check-ins and redeem official gift codes. It doesn't touch anything else.

How do I sign up?

Follow IG @kb4r4, then send a DM with the format please add <UID>. Once your follow is verified, your UID is processed right away. With no payment at all.

You'll be asked two quick questions: (1) do you want to receive a daily report of your check-in and redeem code results via DM, and (2) which language you'd like those reports in. Just answer in your own language. Once you've answered, your UID is processed right away.

No. This service is 100% free. The only requirement is following IG @kb4r4. If you found it helpful and want to support it, you can leave a voluntary tip via QRIS (Indonesia) or USDC (Solana) for crypto users in the tip section. Not required, and they don't affect your service.

What happens if I unfollow / change my IG username?

How long does this service last?

As long as the bot is running, or until you stop playing.
